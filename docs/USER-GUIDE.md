# Claude Machine: User Guide

## Start here: the 5-minute setup

1. **Connect an AI.** Click the gear icon (Settings). Pick **Claude**, **Gemini**, **ChatGPT** or **Grok**, paste that provider's API key, click **Load my models**, choose a model and **Save**. You can add keys for all four and switch at any time.
2. **Add your voice.** Open **LinkedIn Comment Replies** and paste 3-5 comments you've really written into **Your voice**. Every generator in the machine uses them to sound like you.
3. **Fill in your site profiles.** Open each site under **Daily** (Wiredvibe, AIToolboard, ATL-Trust, AlbionLM, VoxSkills) and check its profile. The AI only says what's written there.
4. **Generate.** On each Daily site, press **Plan & write 50 days**. Then open the **Content Calendar**.
5. **Ask the Advisor.** Open **Advisor** for a checklist of what to fix and an AI review of how to grow.

Everything you create is stored in this browser on this computer. Your API keys never leave your browser except to go to the AI provider you chose.

## What's real and what's a demo

Real (makes live AI calls or real files):
- **Daily** sites, **Content Calendar**, **Agents**, **Advisor**, **Weekly AI Automation Newsletter**, **YouTube Channel Builder**, **LinkedIn Comment Replies** (and its Chrome extension), the 105 **Specialists** on the Agent Dashboard, **Design Specialist**, and **Podcast Studio** playback.

Demos (look real but only simulate output; nothing is generated or posted):

Nothing in the machine posts to social media by itself. You post each item through the calendar's links. See "Posting and automation" at the end.

## Settings

- **Provider**: which AI runs things by default. Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Grok. **Demo Mode** shows sample text only and makes no AI calls.
- **Model**: the model for that provider. **Load my models** asks the provider which models your key can use. **Type a model ID** lets you enter any model name exactly.
- **API keys**: one box per provider. Keys are stored in this browser only. Get keys at console.anthropic.com (Claude), aistudio.google.com (Gemini), platform.openai.com (ChatGPT) and console.x.ai (Grok).
- Which provider for what: **news research and the Advisor's web search need Gemini or ChatGPT**. Everything else works with all four.

## The humanizer

Every piece of content passes through the humanizer, which:
- Lays out each prompt as Task, Audience, Rules and Reference sections, with your own text wrapped in tags so the AI never mistakes it for instructions.
- Bans the phrases and patterns that make text read as AI-written: em dashes, "it's not X, it's Y", lists of three, praise openers, words like "delve", "leverage" and "game-changer".
- Checks the result. If anything slips through, it rewrites it once automatically. Anything still flagged is shown in amber so you can fix it or press **Humanize again**.

No tool can guarantee text will pass every AI detector, but this removes the usual giveaways. Your voice samples make the biggest difference.

## Daily: your five sites

Each site under **Daily** has its own page.

**Site profile fields**
- **What it is**: facts only. The AI will not claim anything that isn't here.
- **Who it's for**: the audience the posts speak to.
- **Content themes**: comma-separated topics the 50-day plan rotates through.
- **Tone of voice** and **Call to action**: how posts sound and what they ask people to do.
- **Website**: used in posts and links.
- **LinkedIn company page admin URL**, **Facebook page URL**, **Substack publication URL**, **Website admin URL**: where the calendar's **Open** buttons take you for each platform.

**Generate**
- **First day** and **Days**: the date range (default: tomorrow, 50 days).
- **Plan & write 50 days**: makes a topic plan (one AI call), then writes 5 days per call and saves each batch to the calendar as it arrives. If it stops, press **Continue** to carry on where it left off.
- **Stop after this batch**: stops cleanly; nothing is lost.
- **New topic plan**: replaces the plan for days not yet written.
- **Topic plan**: each day's theme and topic. Click a written day to open it in the calendar.
- **Delete all … content**: removes that site's generated days and plan.

Each day contains: a LinkedIn post by you, a LinkedIn company page post, an X post, a Facebook page post, a TikTok caption, a YouTube Shorts title and description, a website/Substack article, and a short-video plan (opening hook, voiceover, scenes, AI video prompt).

## Content Calendar

**Views**
- **Month**: a grid of every item, colour-coded by site. Click a coloured chip to open it.
- **Upcoming**: a list of everything from today onwards.
- **Filters** (the coloured pills): show or hide a site, the newsletter or agent output.
- **Export CSV**: downloads the visible month (or upcoming list) for a scheduling tool.

**An item's panel**
- **Status**: Draft, Approved or Posted.
- **Copy link to this item**: a permanent link that opens this item directly, in this browser.
- **Platform tabs** (LI, LI Co, X, FB, TT, YT, SS, Site, Video): one version per platform. A tick means you've pasted its live link. An amber outline means the humanizer flagged it.
- **Text box**: edit freely; it saves when you click away. Substack and Site share one article.
- **Copy**: copies that platform's text.
- **Open …**: opens the platform's post editor. LinkedIn and X open with the text already filled in.
- **Humanize**: appears when the text is flagged; rewrites it.
- **Live post URL**: paste the link once posted. When every platform has a link, the item is marked Posted.
- **Video tab**: the hook (first 2 seconds), voiceover script, scene list with on-screen text, and an **AI video prompt** to paste into Veo, Sora, Runway, Kling or CapCut. Record or generate the video, then post it with the TikTok and YouTube captions.

**Newsletter items** open the newsletter screen and have a field for the published LinkedIn article link.
**Agent output items** hold whatever an agent produced, with Copy and a "where it was published" link.

## Daily routine (about 15-20 minutes)

1. Open the **Content Calendar**, **Upcoming** view. Click today's items.
2. For each platform tab: read it, tweak it, **Copy**, **Open**, paste and post, then paste the live link back.
3. For the video: copy the AI video prompt into your video tool, or record the voiceover yourself, then post it with the TikTok and YouTube captions.
4. Once a week, open the **Advisor** and do its top action.

## Weekly AI Automation Newsletter

**Fields**
- **Issue number**, **Issue date**: the number and date shown in the article.
- **News from / News to**: the window searched. Quick buttons: **Last 7 days**, **Last 14 days**, **Since last issue**.
- **Good news from your side of the fence**: your own updates (bullet notes are fine). Only what you write here is used.
- **Podcast episode to feature**: optional title, summary and link for the podcast slot.
- **Format & style**: your byline, sign-off and hashtags (added exactly as written), and the last issue used as the style reference. **Add a SOURCES list** adds the story links at the end.

**Steps**
1. **Search the web**: finds this period's AI news with your AI's web search. Every story shows its sources. Stories marked **checked** had their publisher found by the search and fall inside your dates; anything else starts unticked, so open its links before using it.
2. **Write issue #…**: writes the issue in the house format (Welcome back, Gene's Take with My POV, Our side of the fence, The big stories, Tools worth trying, Quick hits, Podcast, That's a wrap) using only the ticked stories, then runs the humanizer. **Preview** shows it as it will look; **Edit** lets you change the text.
3. **Images**: free AI images appear one at a time (the free service allows one every 45 seconds). **Download** each one, or **New variation** for a different take.
4. **Copy to LinkedIn**: in LinkedIn choose Write article, then your newsletter. Upload the cover, press **Copy title** and paste, then **Copy article** and paste into the body (headings, bold and links keep their formatting). Add the section images, check it, publish.
5. **I've published #…**: archives the issue, makes it the style reference for the next one, and moves on to the next number. Then paste the article's link on its calendar item.

**Friday auto-draft**: if you open the app on a Friday (or that weekend) with a Gemini or ChatGPT key, and this week's issue hasn't been drafted, the machine researches and writes it in the background, puts it on the calendar and shows a banner. It runs only while the app is open, and never publishes.

## Agents

282 specialist agents from The Agency (an open-source, MIT-licensed library), in divisions such as Marketing, Sales, Paid Media, Product, Engineering and Security.

**Fields**
- **Search** and the **division menu**: **★ Recommended for you** shows the agents picked for content, social media, SEO, AI-search visibility and sales.
- **Show this agent's full instructions**: what the agent has been told to be and do.
- **Task**: what you want, as specifically as possible ("Write 5 LinkedIn posts announcing AlbionLM early access to UK security leaders").
- **Brand**: adds that site's profile as context, so the agent knows the facts.
- **AI for this run** and **Model**: run this agent on Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT or Grok, regardless of your default.
- **Extra context**: notes, data, examples, links.
- **Humanize the output**: on for anything you'll publish; off for code, data or plans.

**After a run**: **Copy**, or pick a date and **Add to calendar** so the result sits on your content calendar with its own link. **Recent runs** keeps your last 40 runs.

Good starting tasks:
- **SEO Specialist** with a site selected: "Audit this site's likely search positioning and give me 10 article titles that could rank."
- **AEO Foundations Architect**: "What should this site add so AI assistants can find and cite it? Give me an llms.txt and the changes."
- **Offer & Lead Gen Strategist**: "Design a lead magnet and a 3-email follow-up for this site."
- **TikTok Strategist**: "Plan 10 TikTok videos for this site with hooks."

## Advisor

- **Health check**: worked out on your device, instantly and for free. Amber items need action; **Fix** jumps to the screen that fixes it.
- **AI business review**: one AI call covering what you're not using, which agents to put to work and with what task, website improvements per site, ways to make more money ranked by effort, and a 7-day plan. With Gemini or ChatGPT it also searches the web for your sites and market, and lists what it looked at.

## YouTube Channel Builder

Five linked steps: niche, competitor research and video ideas, a full script, a faceless production workflow, and a 90-day growth plan. Fill in your profile (interests, audience, region, format, hours, budget, how you want to earn) and press **Build my channel**, or run steps one at a time. Each step feeds the next. Paste real competitor findings into step 2 for sharper results. **Download full plan** saves everything as one document. It writes plans; it doesn't make or upload videos.

## LinkedIn Comment Replies and the Chrome extension

- **On the website**: paste posts you've found (link and text, separated by `---`), press **Rank & draft replies**. Each post gets a brand-fit score, the best-matching brand and a humanized reply to copy.
- **The Chrome extension** adds a **✨ Draft reply** button under every LinkedIn post. **Approve** puts the reply in that post's comment box; you press Post. Install it from the download on that screen (unzip, open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, Load unpacked). It reuses your key, brands and voice samples from this app.

## Specialists

The Agent Dashboard holds 105 prompt specialists in 10 categories. Open one, fill in its fields and press Run. Output goes through the humanizer and is saved in **Run History**. **Design Specialist** creates a new specialist from a description.

## Posting and automation

Today the machine **prepares** everything and gives you one-click links to post it; you press Post. Fully automatic posting needs each platform's approved developer access (or a paid posting service such as Ayrshare, Buffer or Blotato) and a small server to keep the access tokens safe. That's the next phase: decide which channels, whether to use a posting service, and whether you approve each post.

## Your data

- Content, profiles, settings and keys are stored in **this browser on this computer**. Another computer or browser starts empty.
- The calendar uses the browser's built-in database (IndexedDB). Clearing your browser's site data deletes it, so **Export CSV** now and then as a backup.
- AI calls go straight from your browser to the provider you chose.

## Troubleshooting

- **"API key … is missing"**: add the key for that provider in Settings.
- **A provider error with a number (400, 401, 404, 429)**: 401 means the key is wrong; 404 usually means the model name is wrong (use **Load my models**); 429 means you've hit the provider's rate limit, so wait a minute.
- **"Claude declined this request"**: Claude's safety filter stopped it. Rephrase, or run that task on another provider.
- **Generation stopped part-way**: press **Continue**; finished days are saved.
- **Images don't appear**: the free image service allows one image every 45 seconds per connection. Wait for the countdown, then try **New variation**.
- **The calendar is empty on another computer**: data lives in the browser where it was created.
